## Part B: Projections (AGI and beyond), status as of Sep 2026

### Takeaway
The picture in Sep 2026 is mixed. Measured capability trends (METR time horizons, compute at 5×/yr, price per capability falling 10–200×/yr, SWE-bench near saturation) are running at or above "steady progress". Yet the forecasters who made the most aggressive calls have themselves pushed back their medians (Kokotajlo 2027→~2030). Interactive and novel-reasoning benchmarks (ARC-AGI-3: frontier LLMs under 1% versus humans at 100%) back the plateau camp's claim that raw benchmark gains are not general intelligence.

### Inferences
- **(Speculation, labelled.)** Three of the four fast-takeoff anchors (AI 2027, Amodei's 2026, Altman's 2026 "novel insights") have target dates at or near now. Their authors have hedged or revised (Kokotajlo explicitly). That makes a strong "check the receipts" section for Unhyped.
- The core tension in the data: task-horizon, coding and price trends fit fast progress, while ARC-AGI-3 fits the view that learning-efficiency generalization is not being solved by scale. Both camps can cite 2026 evidence honestly.
- Within the "steady" scenario, Hassabis moved his date sooner while Kokotajlo moved his later. Forecasters are converging on a ~2029–2033 window.

### Gaps
- The Metaculus figures (Jan 2033 strong / ~Sep 2027 weak) came from secondary aggregators. The Metaculus page blocked fetching.
- Current ARC-AGI-1/2/3 leaderboard numbers for Sep 2026 could not be confirmed. The "GPT-6 Astra" claim is unverified and possibly erroneous.
- There are no exact METR hour values for models after May 2026, and no confirmed current doubling estimate from METR itself (only third-party analyses).
- Epoch's long-run AGI and compute projections to 2030 (e.g. "Can AI scaling continue through 2030?") were not fetched.
- No verbatim recent quote was pulled from LeCun or Marcus for the projection entries. Their positions are described from secondary summaries.
- Stanford AI Index 2026 figures come from HAI summary snippets. Chapter-level figures (e.g. inference cost decline, training compute of the top model) were not pulled.
