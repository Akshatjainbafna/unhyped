Legend for quote status:

- **VERBATIM (verify)**: widely reported verbatim, but I did not open the primary video or transcript. Check it before publishing.
- **PARAPHRASE: DO NOT USE**: only a paraphrase was found.

Source-quality note: many 2026 results came from low-quality aggregator/SEO blogs (e.g., taskade, tech-insider.org, explainx.ai, openclawvps, layer3labs). Where I could only find those, I flag it. Prefer the Bloomberg/Reuters/CNBC/TechCrunch/SEC links below.

## Part A: Claim-vs-Reality pairs

### Takeaway
Across ~50 documented pairs, the pattern is consistent. Specific timelines ("next year", "within 5 years", "3–6 months") were usually wrong or only partly true. Directional claims about AI coding and agent capability often came broadly true, but more slowly and less completely than the headline said. Consumer AI hardware launched on big promises failed almost every time (Humane, Rabbit, Friend, Sora app). Some bold claims did come true (AlphaFold, Waymo-style robotaxis at scale, AI at IMO gold level, AI coding revenue, Amazon/Salesforce headcount cuts). Some famous *dismissals* of AI impact were wrong (Chegg CEO calling the ChatGPT threat "extraordinarily overblown"; "deep learning is hitting a wall").

#### A1. Hinton on radiologists (2016)
- related_entry: `deep-learning-radiology`
- claim: "If you work as a radiologist, you're like the coyote that's already over the edge of the cliff but hasn't yet looked down" (VERBATIM, verified via Fortune). The longer, widely cited continuation is "People should stop training radiologists now. It's just completely obvious that within five years deep learning is going to do better than radiologists." (VERBATIM (verify) against the Creative Destruction Lab / "Machine Learning and the Market for Intelligence" video, Toronto, 2016.) — [Fortune, 2026-07-19](https://fortune.com/article/ai-godfather-radiologists-obsolete-salaries-up-to-571k-demand-growing/)
- speaker: Geoffrey Hinton, deep-learning pioneer (Google/U of Toronto at the time). Date: 2016 (Oct/Nov, Toronto conference).
- reality: A decade later, radiology is one of the hardest US specialties to staff. Average radiologist pay reached $571K in 2025 (+9% YoY). There were 7,469 active radiology job postings in May 2026, and 1,470 of them had been open more than 60 days. Radiology caseload rose about 25% from 2018 to early 2025. — [Fortune, 2026-07-19](https://fortune.com/article/ai-godfather-radiologists-obsolete-salaries-up-to-571k-demand-growing/); [CNN, 2026-02-09](https://www.cnn.com/2026/02/09/tech/ai-replacing-jobs-concerns-radiology)
- Hinton walk-back: on X he wrote "Yes, I was wrong about the time scale, but AI is already comparable with radiologists for several types of images" (VERBATIM, verified, ~Nov 2023) — [X/@geoffreyhinton](https://x.com/geoffreyhinton/status/1719451766599659591). In 2025 he told the NYT's Steve Lohr that he "spoke too broadly" and now expects a "combination of A.I. and a radiologist" — [AuntMinnie](https://www.auntminnie.com/imaging-informatics/artificial-intelligence/article/15746014/hinton-acknowledges-mistake-in-predicting-ai-replacement-of-radiologists)
- status: **wrong** (on timing and on the jobs outcome)

#### A8. Amodei: white-collar "bloodbath" (May 2025)

It named a new CEO (Ash Ashutosh) in Sept 2025 and remained independent into 2026 — [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/top-funded-ai-database-startup-pinecone-considers-sale); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rz31q82b5); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/from-shiny-object-to-sober-reality-the-vector-database-story-two-years-later)
- status: **partially** (standalone category squeezed; vector search is everywhere as a feature)

### Inferences
- **Timelines fail more than directions.** Hinton, Musk, Amodei (90%), Zuckerberg and Mostaque mostly got the direction right and the dates wrong. For the dataset's "X killed Y" thesis, the strongest point is how much the dates missed by, not that the claims were baseless.
- **Consumer AI hardware is the clearest flop category.** Humane, Rabbit and Friend all had big launches, few units sold, and quick collapses. The Sora app (2025–26) shows a well-funded software product can fail fast too.
- **"Fake AI" stories share a pattern.** Duplex, Just Walk Out and Builder.ai all involved humans in the loop being presented as automation. The nuance matters: for Builder.ai the proven fraud was revenue misstatement.
- **Several bold claims did come true** and should be included for even-handedness: AlphaFold, IMO gold, Salesforce/Amazon headcount cuts, coding-agent revenue (Claude Code, Cognition), and Siri eventually shipping. Chegg is a counter-case where the *dismissal* of AI risk was wrong.
- **Stock prices vs. fundamentals.** The 2026 SaaS selloff shows "X killed Y" narratives now move markets (Figma −7% on Claude Design) even while the "killed" companies report record revenue (Adobe, Salesforce).

## Part B: "Still alive" follow-ups

### Takeaway
Most things declared "killed" by AI were still alive and often growing by Sept 2026: Google Search, Adobe/Photoshop, SaaS revenue, LangChain, RAG as a technique, radiologists. The honest exceptions matter. Stack Overflow's *public Q&A* volume did collapse (down about 99% from its peak). Chegg is in steep decline. Customer-service rep employment is falling. Entry-level hiring for young workers in AI-exposed jobs is measurably weaker. Standalone vector DBs and coding bootcamps shrank. Graphic design and translation are under pressure but not "dead".

### Cited Findings

#### B1. Stack Overflow (declared dead)
- Declared: The Pragmatic Engineer, "Stack overflow is almost dead" (Gergely Orosz, 2025) — [Pragmatic Engineer](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/stack-overflow-is-almost-dead/)
- Actual decline, stated fairly: monthly questions peaked around 200K (2014). They fell to 17,935 in Dec 2024, 3,312 in Dec 2025 (−81.5% YoY), 1,072 in Jun 2026 (the all-time low), and 1,304 in Jul 2026 — [DevClass 2026-01-05](https://www.devclass.com/ai-ml/2026/01/05/dramatic-drop-in-stack-overflow-questions-as-devs-look-elsewhere-for-help/4079575); [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/devops/137686/stack-overflow-in-freefall-78-percent-drop-in-number-of-questions/); [KuCoin news flash (low-quality; the numbers match other outlets)](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/stack-overflows-monthly-questions-drop-to-1-304-in-july-2026-down-99-from-2014)
- Still alive as a business: revenue was about $115M for FY to Mar 2025, driven by Teams/enterprise and AI data-licensing deals (OpenAI, Google). It launched pay-per-crawl with Cloudflare (Feb 2026) and a "Stack Overflow for Agents" beta — [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/stack-overflows-plan-to-survive-the-age-of-ai/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_Overflow). Prosus reportedly cut the valuation of an AI-hit subsidiary by more than half (likely Stack Overflow; the article was paywalled/403 and not confirmed) — [Moneyweb](https://www.moneyweb.co.za/news/companies-and-deals/prosus-slashes-valuation-of-ai-hit-subsidiary-by-more-than-half/)
- Verdict: **the community Q&A is effectively dead; the company is alive in a smaller form.**

#### B2. Google Search
- Declared: Paul Buchheit, 2022-12-01 (see A41).
- Actual: Q2 2026 Search & other revenue +17% YoY (Q1 2026 +19%). Pichai says "popular AI features are driving Search query growth". Gemini App has 950M MAU — [Alphabet Q2 2026](https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/2026q2-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-search-revenue-growth-eases-after-a-year-of-acceleration/583181/)
- Verdict: **alive and growing.** Publishers' click-through traffic is a separate question and was not researched here.

#### B3. Junior developers / entry-level white-collar
- Declared: Amodei (A8), Huang (A11), Mostaque (A12).
- Actual: Stanford DEL (Aug 2026) found employment of 22–25-year-olds in the most AI-exposed occupations is 19% below the counterfactual. In the two most exposed quintiles it fell about 11% from Nov 2022 to Jun 2026, while the same age group in the least-exposed quintiles grew about 10%. There is no comparable gap for experienced workers and no widespread displacement — [Stanford DEL](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26/); [paper PDF](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/app/uploads/2026/08/Canaries_August2026.pdf). SignalFire 2025: new-grad hiring at the 15 largest tech firms was down more than 50% vs 2019, and new grads were 7% of Big Tech hires — [SignalFire 2025](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025). Stanford also has a paper on interest-rate confounds — [Stanford DEL](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canaries-interest-rates-and-timinga-more-on-recent-drivers-of-employment-changes-for-young-workers/)
- Verdict: **real, measurable decline for entry-level roles, not extinction.** Interest rates and the post-2022 tech correction are confounds.

#### B4. Photoshop / Adobe
- Declared: the "Claude Design killed Figma/Photoshop" narrative (Apr 2026), plus a long-running "Is Adobe dead?" discourse — [NoGood newsletter](https://newsletter.nogood.io/p/is-adobe-dead-figma-fans-rise-up)
- Actual: Adobe Q3 FY26 (quarter ended Aug 28, 2026) revenue was a record $6.76B (+13%). Total ARR is $27.5B (+11.2%), AI-first ARR is above $650M, and full-year FY26 guidance was raised to about $26.6B. A CEO transition was announced in the same period — [Adobe 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000796343/000079634326000156/adbe-20260828.htm); [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/adobe-q3-fy2026-slides-strong-results-ceo-transition-announced-93CH-4897031). The stock is down sharply, reported at −45% by one secondary source — [SaaS Sentinel](https://saassentinel.com/2026/07/03/saaspocalypse-2026-what-happened-to-saas-and-where-the-market-stands-now/)
- Verdict: **the business is alive and growing; market sentiment is the part that got hit.**

#### B5. Chegg
- Declared: effectively by its own CEO in May 2023 (the ChatGPT spike) and by the market (−48% in one day).
- Actual: headcount cut 22% (May 2025) and 45% (Oct 2025). Subscribers were 3.2M in Q1 2025 (−31% YoY). The stock is down about 99% from its peak. Q2 2026 revenue was reportedly $51.8M (−51%) — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/27/chegg-slashes-45percent-of-workforce-blames-new-realities-of-ai.html); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/petercohan/2025/10/29/chegg-stock-down-99-learn-whether-ai-45-layoffs-make-chgg-a-buy/); [Chegg FY2025 release](https://investor.chegg.com/Press-Releases/press-release-details/2026/Chegg-Reports-2025-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Financial-Results/default.aspx)
- Verdict: **genuinely in decline.** This is the clearest real AI casualty in the set.

#### B6. Customer-support jobs
- Declared: Klarna (A30), Salesforce (A31).
- Actual: BLS OEWS shows US customer service representative employment fell by 130,180 (−4.8%) in the year to May 2025, the second straight year of losses. BLS projects −5% for 2025–2035 but still about 289,500 openings a year — [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/office-and-administrative-support/customer-service-representatives.htm); [Detroit News 2026-05-16](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/business/2026/05/16/ai-artificial-intelligence-job-losses-customer-service-sales-secretaries/90118520007/). Klarna partly reversed its all-AI approach (A30).
- Verdict: **declining, not dead.**

#### B7. Translators
- Declared: common in commentary; no single high-profile verbatim speaker captured (gap).
- Actual: BLS projects +2% for interpreters and translators over 2025–2035, with median wage $60,170 (May 2025). They are among 18 AI-exposed occupations that fell 0.2% in aggregate (May 2024–May 2025). In a UK Society of Authors survey (Jan 2024), 36% of translators said they had already lost work to genAI and 43% reported lower income — [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/media-and-communication/interpreters-and-translators.htm); [Slator](https://slator.com/us-bureau-of-labor-statistics-job-outlook-for-translators-interpreters-worsens/)
- Verdict: **under real pressure on income and freelance work; the occupation still exists.**

#### B8. RAG
- Declared: a diffuse "RAG is dead" meme after Gemini 1.5's 1M-token context (Feb 2024); no named high-profile speaker (gap) — [Vectorize](https://vectorize.io/blog/rag-is-dead-long-live-rag); [LightOn](https://lighton.ai/lighton-blogs/rag-is-dead-long-live-rag-retrieval-in-the-age-of-agents)
- Actual: retrieval turned into agentic search/tool use rather than disappearing. The rise of "grep + long context" in coding agents is a partial truth for the "mandatory vector DB" version of RAG — [AkitaOnRails 2026-04-06](https://akitaonrails.com/en/2026/04/06/rag-is-dead-long-context/)
- Verdict: **alive, changed form.**

#### B9. LangChain
- Declared: a recurring community meme, "LangChain is dead" — [Agentic Engineering Institute, Jul 2026](https://www.agenticengineeringinstitute.com/blog/langchain-is-dead-what-serious-ai-agent-teams-are-actually-using-in-july-2026)
- Actual: raised $125M at a $1.25B valuation in Oct 2025 (IVP lead; CapitalG, Sapphire, Sequoia, Benchmark) — [TechCrunch 2025-10-21](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/21/open-source-agentic-startup-langchain-hits-1-25b-valuation); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/10/20/exclusive-early-ai-darling-langchain-is-now-a-unicorn-with-a-fresh-125-million-in-funding/). Developer mindshare shifted toward provider-native SDKs, agent harnesses and MCP.
- Verdict: **alive commercially; less central technically.**

#### B10. Vector DBs
- Declared: Elastic CEO (A50).
- Actual: Pinecone explored a sale, changed CEO and remained independent. Postgres/pgvector, Elastic, MongoDB and the cloud providers all offer vector search — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/from-shiny-object-to-sober-reality-the-vector-database-story-two-years-later)
- Verdict: **the standalone category is squeezed; vector search is ubiquitous as a feature.**

#### B11. SaaS
- Declared: Nadella (A40); the "SaaSpocalypse" (Feb 2026).
- Actual: stocks were down heavily, but Salesforce revenue grew +11% to $11.3B with guidance raised to $46.1–46.4B for FY27, and Agentforce plus Data Cloud AI ARR is about $3.9B (+210%) — [Salesforce Q2 FY27](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/fy27-q2-earnings/). Adobe revenue grew +13% (B4).
- Verdict: **revenue alive; seat-based valuations repriced.**

#### B12. Graphic designers
- Declared: generic commentary; WEF Future of Jobs 2025 lists graphic designers among the fastest-declining roles (11th).
- Actual: BLS projects −2% for 2025–2035 with about 16,000 openings a year — [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/graphic-designers.htm). A Ramp Economics Lab study (Feb 2026) reportedly shows spending on graphic design down 17% (secondary, not verified). Upwork says graphic design demand stayed "consistently strong" — [Upwork In-Demand Skills 2026](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai)
- Verdict: **pressured, mixed data.**

#### B13. Coding bootcamps
- Declared: implicitly by Huang (A11) and Mostaque (A12).
- Actual: 2U wound down its university bootcamps (Dec 2024). Turing School suspended enrollment (Apr 2025) and its founder announced intent to file for bankruptcy. Codeup, Kenzie and Launch Academy closed in 2023–24. Codesmith pivoted to AI leadership programs — [Inside Higher Ed 2025-01-09](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/tech-innovation/teaching-learning/2025/01/09/changes-boot-camp-marks-signal-shifts-workforce); [Wikipedia: Codesmith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Codesmith)
- Verdict: **genuine contraction.** It started before genAI with the 2022–23 tech hiring slump, and AI accelerated it.

#### B14. Radiologists
- Declared: Hinton 2016 (A1).
- Actual: salaries and demand are at record levels (see A1).
- Verdict: **alive and scarce.**

#### B15. Duolingo / language-learning apps (bonus)
- Declared: "AI will kill language apps" is a common take; no named verbatim speaker captured.
- Actual: Q1 2026 DAUs +21% and revenue +27% (secondary source) — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/duolingo-went-ai-first-then-070000682.html)
- Verdict: **alive.**

### Inferences
- The fair scorecard reads "declined, not dead" for Stack Overflow Q&A, Chegg, customer-support jobs, bootcamps, entry-level devs and standalone vector DBs. It reads "alive and growing" for Google Search, Adobe, Salesforce/SaaS revenue, LangChain, radiologists and Duolingo.
- Businesses that lived on answering questions (Chegg, Stack Overflow's public Q&A) were hit hardest. Businesses that own workflows or distribution (Adobe, Salesforce, Google) absorbed AI into their own products and kept growing revenue, even as their stock multiples compressed.
- The entry-level labor signal is the most serious real effect. It is concentrated in young workers in exposed occupations and is confounded by interest rates.

### Gaps
- Named, verbatim "X is dead" declarers are missing for translators, graphic designers, RAG, LangChain, customer support and Duolingo. Most are diffuse social-media memes.
- Stack Overflow site traffic (Similarweb) for 2026 was not captured; only question volume. The Prosus valuation-cut article could not be fetched (403).
- Chegg Q2 2026 revenue ($51.8M, −51%) came from a search summary. Verify against the Chegg 8-K.
- BLS software-developer employment for 2025–26 was not pulled; nor were Google publisher click-through data or Stanford AI Index 2026 figures.
- The IGV −30% and per-stock drawdowns (Figma −76%, Adobe −45%) come from low-quality secondary blogs. Verify with Bloomberg/Reuters market data.
